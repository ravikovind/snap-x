# Showcase

Real (and fictional-demo) packs made with snap-x. If you've used it for your own project, channel, app or store listing, add yours below.

## Submit yours

Open a PR that adds a row to the table below, with:

- A real rendered image from your pack (not a mockup of one).
- A link to the repo, channel, app, or site it's for.
- Confirmation that you own the brand shown, or have permission to show it (a fictional demo brand, clearly labeled as such, is also welcome — see `examples/storefront` and `examples/creator-series` for the pattern).
