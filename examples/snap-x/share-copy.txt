    example packs (ravikovind, heyreach, open-notifier, food-app-banners, diwali-poster) picked
    for aspect-ratio and palette variety. Two orientations, same asset set (see
    designs/_li-announce.mjs) — the portrait one is a real re-layout, not a resize, since LinkedIn
    itself recommends 4:5 for feed posts (more mobile-feed space, better engagement) over the
    1200×627 link-preview shape. Pair with a text post and, optionally,
    examples/ravikovind/guides/ravi-kovind-linkedin-cover.guides.png as a second image showing
    the danger-zone check on an actual LinkedIn cover.
